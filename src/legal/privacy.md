# Privacy Policy

**Version 2026-08-10**

This explains what **[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]** ("we") collects when you use
Been-go!, why, and what you can do about it.

The short version: we collect what running a marketplace requires and nothing
extra. We do not sell your data. We do not run advertising trackers.

---

## 1. What we collect

**You give us:**

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Email address | Sign in, order notifications, account recovery |
| Display name and optional bio | So other members know who they are dealing with |
| Password | Stored only as a hash, by our auth provider — we never see it |
| Listing content: titles, descriptions, photos, prices | To show your listings |
| Offer messages | To pass them to the other party |
| Invite notes and the email you label an invite with | So you remember who a code was for |

**Stripe collects, and we never see:**

- Your card number, CVC, or bank account details
- A seller's government ID, tax ID, or date of birth

Stripe passes back only what we need to run the order: whether a seller's
account can accept charges and payouts, an opaque account identifier, and the
shipping address and name a buyer entered at checkout.

**Generated automatically:**

- Order records: what was bought, from whom, for how much, when
- Which terms version you accepted, and when
- Listing view counts (aggregate; we do not record who viewed what)
- Standard server and security logs held by our infrastructure providers

**We do not collect:** your location, your contacts, your browsing on other
sites, or anything from advertising networks. There are no third-party
analytics or advertising trackers in this application.

## 2. Why we can use it (legal bases)

Where the GDPR or a similar law applies to you, our bases are:

- **Contract** — running your account, listings, and orders.
- **Legal obligation** — tax, accounting, and anti-fraud record-keeping.
- **Legitimate interests** — keeping the marketplace safe, preventing fraud and
  abuse, and defending legal claims.
- **Consent** — anything optional, which you can withdraw at any time.

## 3. Who else sees your data

We use these processors. Each is bound to handle your data only on our
instructions.

| Provider | What it handles |
|---|---|
| **Supabase** | Database, authentication, photo storage |
| **Stripe** | All payments, seller identity verification, payouts |
| **Cloudflare** | Hosting and delivery of the website |

We share data with other members only where the transaction requires it:

- **Sellers see** the buyer's name and shipping address, **after payment**, and
  only for that order. Using it for anything but shipping that order breaks the
  Community Rules.
- **Buyers see** the seller's display name and bio.
- **Nobody sees** your email address, unless you put it in a message yourself.
- **Offers are visible only to the buyer and the seller.** Other members cannot
  see what anyone offered.

We will disclose data to law enforcement where we are legally required to, or
where we reasonably believe it is necessary to prevent serious harm. Where we
are permitted to tell you, we will.

**We do not sell your personal information**, and we do not share it for
cross-context behavioural advertising.

## 4. How long we keep it

| | Retention |
|---|---|
| Account and profile | While your account is open |
| Listings and photos | Until you delete them |
| Order records | **7 years** after the order — tax and accounting requirements |
| Terms acceptances | 7 years — this is the record that you agreed |
| Moderation records and reports | Up to 3 years |
| Suspension records | Indefinitely, so a removed member is not re-invited |

When you close your account we delete or anonymise your profile and listings,
but we keep the order and tax records above. We cannot delete a completed
transaction: the other party has a legitimate interest in their own receipt, and
we have legal obligations attached to it.

## 5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you
- **Correct** anything inaccurate
- **Delete** your data, subject to the retention rules above
- **Export** your data in a portable format
- **Object to** or **restrict** certain processing
- **Withdraw consent** where consent is the basis
- **Not be discriminated against** for exercising any of these

Email **[SUPPORT EMAIL]** and we will respond within **30 days**. We may need to
verify your identity first — usually by confirming you control the account's
email address.

If you are in the EU or UK and are unhappy with our response, you may complain
to your national data protection authority.

## 6. Cookies and local storage

We use **no advertising or analytics cookies**.

We store the following in your browser, because the app cannot work without it:

| What | Why | Lifetime |
|---|---|---|
| Session token | Keeps you signed in | Until you sign out |
| Invite code you typed at signup | So you do not retype it after confirming your email | Cleared once redeemed |

## 7. Security

- Passwords are hashed by our auth provider; we never store or see them.
- Everything is served over HTTPS.
- Access to your data is enforced in the database itself with row-level security
  rules, not only in the application — so a bug in the website cannot expose
  another member's orders.
- Payment credentials never reach our servers.

No system is perfectly secure. If we discover a breach affecting your personal
data, we will notify you and the relevant regulator as the law requires.

## 8. Children

Been-go! is for adults. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not
knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has an account,
tell us at **[SUPPORT EMAIL]** and we will remove it.

## 9. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the
United States. Where personal data is transferred out of the UK or EEA, it is
covered by an approved transfer mechanism such as the Standard Contractual
Clauses.

## 10. Changes

We will post any change here with a new version date, and tell you in the app if
the change is material.
