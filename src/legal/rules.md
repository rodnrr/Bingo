# Community Rules & Prohibited Items

**Version 2026-08-10** — these rules form part of the Terms of Service.

Been-go! works because everyone here was vouched for by someone already here.
That is the whole safety model. These rules are how we keep it worth something.

---

## The short version

1. **Sell what you say you're selling.** Honest condition, honest photos,
   honest flaws.
2. **Ship what you sold, quickly.** Within 5 business days of payment.
3. **Pay for what you bought.** An accepted offer is a commitment.
4. **Be decent.** Members are people your friends vouched for.
5. **Nothing on the prohibited list.** No exceptions, no "but mine is
   different".
6. **Invite people you'd stand behind.** Your invites are your reputation.

---

## Prohibited items

Do not list any of the following. This list is not exhaustive — if something is
obviously in the spirit of this list, it is prohibited too. If you are unsure,
ask at **[SUPPORT EMAIL]** before listing.

### Weapons and dangerous goods

- Firearms, frames, receivers, and completed lowers
- Ammunition, magazines, and reloading components
- Explosives, fireworks, flares, blasting caps
- Silencers, suppressors, conversion devices, automatic-fire parts
- Switchblades, ballistic knives, and knives illegal where you are
- Body armour where its sale is restricted
- Tasers, stun guns, and pepper spray where restricted

### Drugs and related

- Illegal drugs and controlled substances of any kind
- Drug paraphernalia
- Prescription medication, including pet medication
- Vapes, e-liquids, tobacco, and nicotine products
- Alcohol
- Unapproved supplements making medical claims, or anything sold as a cure

### Living things and their remains

- Live animals of any kind
- Human remains, tissue, blood, fluids, or organs
- Endangered species, ivory, and any product from a protected species
- Regulated plants and seeds

### Stolen, counterfeit, and fraudulent

- Stolen property, or anything you cannot prove you have the right to sell
- Counterfeits, replicas, knock-offs, and unauthorised copies
- Pirated media, software, and cracked licences
- Anything with a removed, altered, or obscured serial number
- Devices reported lost or stolen, or locked to another account (iCloud, FRP,
  carrier lock, MDM)

### Financial and personal data

- Personal data, mailing lists, and databases of people
- Credit cards, bank accounts, and payment credentials
- Stocks, securities, and investment instruments
- Cryptocurrency and NFTs
- Gift cards, coupons, vouchers, and store credit
- Lottery tickets, raffles, sweepstakes entries, and anything resembling
  gambling
- Cash, precious metal bullion, and currency sold above face value

### Regulated, hazardous, and recalled

- Prescription medical devices, contact lenses, and hearing aids
- Recalled products of any kind
- Hazardous materials: asbestos, mercury, radioactive material, industrial
  chemicals, pesticides
- Automotive airbags, seat belts, and safety-critical salvage parts
- Devices designed to defeat emissions controls, tolls, or safety systems
- Government, police, and military identification, badges, and uniforms

### Adult and harmful content

- Pornography and sexual services
- Anything sexualising minors, in any form, ever
- Material promoting hate, violence, or terrorism
- Nazi and other hate-group memorabilia sold as an endorsement rather than as a
  documented historical artefact

### Services and intangibles

- Been-go! invite codes — an invite is a vouch, not merchandise
- Accounts on any platform, including this one
- Labour, services, rentals, and subscriptions
- Anything you do not physically have in your possession right now

## Listing rules

- **One item per listing.** Bundles are fine; unrelated items in one listing are
  not.
- **Photos must be of the actual item.** No stock photos, no photos taken from
  another seller's listing. Where the item has damage, photograph the damage.
- **No keyword stuffing.** The title describes what it is. It is not a place to
  list ten brands you are not selling.
- **Price it honestly.** No bait prices, no "message me for the real price", no
  listing an accessory at the price of the item.
- **Shipping cost is shipping cost.** Do not move the item's price into
  shipping to duck the platform fee. This is the one rule we will suspend for on
  a first offence, because it is never accidental.
- **Keep it on Been-go!.** Do not use listings to push buyers to another
  platform or to a cash deal off-site. If someone found your item here, sell it
  here.

## Conduct rules

- **No harassment.** Not in offer messages, not anywhere.
- **No sharing anyone's private information**, including addresses that came to
  you as part of an order. A shipping address is given to you to ship to, and
  for nothing else.
- **No retaliation.** Reporting a rule-breaking listing is not something to
  punish someone for.
- **No manipulation.** No fake offers, no second accounts, no buying your own
  listings.

## Shipping rules

- Ship within **5 business days** of payment.
- Add tracking whenever it is available.
- Package it properly. "It broke in the post" is usually a packing problem.
- Ship to the address on the order and nowhere else. A buyer asking you to
  reroute a package after payment is one of the oldest fraud patterns there is —
  refuse, and report it.

## What happens when a rule is broken

We look at intent and at harm. Roughly:

| | Typical response |
|---|---|
| Honest mistake, minor | Listing removed, a note explaining why |
| Repeated after warning | Listings removed, invites revoked |
| Dishonesty about an item | Suspension |
| Prohibited item, fraud, stolen goods, harassment | Immediate and permanent removal |
| Anything involving a minor | Immediate removal and referral to law enforcement |

Suspension takes your listings down immediately. Orders already paid for are
still yours to fulfil.

**Invites cut both ways.** If someone you invited turns out to be a serious
problem, we may look at your account too. Not to punish you for someone else's
choices — but the invite tree is the trust model, and it only works if a vouch
means something.

## Reporting

Every listing has a **Report** link. You can also email **[SUPPORT EMAIL]**.

Tell us what you saw and, if you can, why it matters. Reports are not visible to
the person you reported.

If you believe an item is stolen, or a member is in danger, contact your local
authorities first. Then tell us.
